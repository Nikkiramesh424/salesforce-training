# Agentforce Summary

Agentforce is Salesforce's platform for building AI-powered agents capable of understanding requests, reasoning through problems, and executing actions across enterprise systems.

Unlike traditional chatbots, Agentforce can:

- Understand user intent
- Use business data
- Execute actions
- Trigger Flows
- Invoke Apex logic
- Interact with enterprise applications

Agentforce combines AI reasoning with enterprise automation to improve productivity and decision-making.

# AI Workflow Thinking

## Enterprise AI Workflow

User Question
↓
AI Agent
↓
Flow / Apex Logic
↓
Database Access
↓
Response Generation
↓
Action Execution

### Workflow Explanation

1. A user submits a request.
2. The AI agent analyzes intent.
3. Agentforce determines which action or process is needed.
4. Salesforce Flow or Apex executes business logic.
5. Data is retrieved from enterprise systems.
6. AI generates a contextual response.
7. The required action is executed automatically.

This architecture combines reasoning, automation, and enterprise data.

# Risks of Enterprise AI

## Hallucinations

AI may generate incorrect information that appears convincing.

## Wrong Automation

Incorrect actions may be executed due to poor instructions or logic.

## Privacy Risks

Sensitive information may be exposed if security controls are weak.

## Bias

AI decisions may reflect biases present in training data.

## Incorrect Approvals

AI may incorrectly recommend approvals or rejections.

## Over-Automation

Excessive automation can reduce human oversight and increase operational risk.

# Revision Questions

## 1. What is an AI agent?
An AI agent is a system that can reason, make decisions, and perform actions to achieve goals.

## 2. How is Agentforce different from a chatbot?
Agentforce can execute actions and integrate with enterprise systems, while chatbots primarily provide responses.

## 3. Why do AI agents need enterprise data?
Enterprise data allows agents to provide accurate and context-aware responses.

## 4. Why should AI systems have guardrails?
Guardrails reduce risks and ensure responsible operation.

## 5. What risks exist in autonomous systems?
Hallucinations, privacy risks, bias, and incorrect automation.

## 6. Why should enterprises carefully validate AI actions?
To ensure accuracy, compliance, and business reliability.

## 7. How can AI integrate with Flows and Apex?
AI agents can invoke Flows and Apex actions to execute business processes.

## 8. Why is AI becoming important in enterprise software?
It improves efficiency, automation, productivity, and decision support.
